Modern Modular Engineering & Construction Company (MMEC) was founded in 2017, began construction phase I in 2019, and commenced fabrication operations in late 2020. As an international supplier of industrial structural steel and modularized solutions, MMEC serves the Energy, Oil & Gas, Chemical Processes, Mining, and Heavy Construction industries.

MMEC operates alongside sister-company Modern Heavy Industries (MDHI) as part of Modern Group, a more extensive, family-owned network of structural steel engineering companies. The owner's goal of MMEC and MDHI was to serve the global community with high-quality products in a world-class setting. To this end, the management caliber, character, project management, transparency, communication, and business ethics has remained the driving force of Modern Group's success.

Modern Modular Engineering & Construction (Suzhou), or MMEC, is located in Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu Province, China, 150km northwest of Shanghai, near Changzhou, Wuxi, and Suzhou. (See Map). The location is accessible by air and high-speed rail. MMEC's business focus is steel structures and modular plants for heavy industries. In addition, the MMEC workshop is adjacent to the Yangtze River Heavy Equipment Port.

Modern Modular Engineering & Construction's Shanghai business office is located at 183 Kaibin Road, Building B, Suite #507, Xuhui District, Shanghai. Office hours are 08:30-17:00, Monday thru Friday.

Modern Heavy Industries (Taicang), MDHI, is located in Taicang City, Jiangsu Province, China, 45km northwest of Shanghai, near Suzhou and Kunshan (See Map). The location is accessible by air and high-speed rail. MDHI's business focus is heavy steel structures for most industries. In addition, the MDHI workshop is 9 kilometers from Taicang Port and 20km from Shanghai Luojing Port.

MMEC supplies fabricated structural steel and piping spools for modules of various industrial applications. Below is a product overview.

  • Hot-rolled steel profile fabrication, including H-Beams, channels, angles, schedule Pipe, and hollow steel sections.
  • Built-up steel sections, box columns, complex assemblies, heavy girders, trusses, nodes, and jumbo steel sections.
  • Carbon steel and stainless-steel piping, duplex & super-duplex, copper-nickel, valves, actuators, instruments, and accessories.
  • Architectural elements, facades, mechanical plate work, stacks, vessels, casing, ducts, chutes, hoppers, liners, wear plates.
  • Bulk conveyors, transfer stations, gallery, overland truss, pipe racks, offshore dolphins, platforms, access steel, handrails, ladders, and grating.
  • Modular process skids, modular pipe racks, pre-assembled units, construction modules, furnace modules, air-cooled heat exchangers, wellhead skids, manifold skids, separation skids, water treatment modules, complex process modules.

MMEC facilities supply large and heavy structures and modules for various global projects.

  • Thermal Power Plants - Coal-Fired, Natural Gas-Fired (OCGT, CCGT/HRSG), Biomass, Waste-to-Energy, Biogas, Geothermal.
  • Renewable Power Plants - Hydropower Plant Structures, Solar Thermal Plant Structures, Tidal Power Equipment.
  • Construction - Sports Stadiums, Arenas, Major Venue, High-Rise Structures, Complex Steel Structures, Theme Park Structures, Typical Buildings.
  • Mining Plants - Above and below ground ore and mineral mining plant structures and mining modules.
  • Oil & Gas Plants - Well Structures, Tank Structures, Natural Gas Plants, Gathering Stations, and Oil Refineries.
  • Chemical & petrochemical plant structures and modularized products.
  • Water Treatment Process

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can our team communicate well with yours?
MMEC communication channel members are a multinational team fluent in English and several other languages.
Can I receive more details and a brochure?
Please download Brochures, Presentations, and other information designated for public release from the 'Media' page. In addition, please inquire through the contact page if you wish to request hard copies of the brochure.
Do you use Tekla Structures for detailing?
MMEC owns 13 Tekla licenses and 4 Tekla Share licenses. In addition to this, we partner with several top detailing firms that hold 60 additional licenses. MMEC can receive most drawing file types. Please refer to the 'Engineering' section above or inquire through the contact page for more information.
How long does it take to provide a cost estimate?
Typically, it takes one week to complete a steel structure estimate after the customer provides all information in the RFQ. However, for modular projects, the completion time is usually two weeks.
How many fitters and welders do you have?
MMEC employs 195 fitters and 180 welders. The welders are qualified for ASW and EN requirements. In addition, if required for particular projects, welders will complete certification for other standards such as AU/NZ, JIS, and GOST. The total number of MMEC staff exceeds 950.
What is your production capacity and lead time?
MMEC manufacturing output exceeds 7000 metric tons per month. For structural steel packages, new projects require approximately 75 to 90 days to begin deliveries, based on engineering completion and the choice of raw materials. Modular project lead time varies due to supply scope, size, and complexity. Please inquire if you require a consultation.
Can you procure foreign standard steel materials?
Although we can fabricate to any national standard, buying foreign steel profiles can often be problematic. China operates modernized steel mills that produce half the world's steel. As such, the government does not promote importing foreign steel stocks. Having a Chinese mill make custom heats for your profiles may be practical if you have large quantities. However, converting your project to utilize local grades and shapes is more practical and efficient if you have small quantities.
What certificates or qualifications does MMEC have?
MMEC has attained ISO 9001, 14001, and 45001. The shop is certified AISC Building Fabricator + Sophisticated Paint, EN 1090-1-2 (EXC-4), EN-ISO 3834, and CSA/CWB 47.1. Certificates can be viewed on the 'Media' page. In addition, MMEC contracts workshop ASME fabrication when specified in the contract.
Can we video-conference with you?
MMEC utilizes video conferencing for introduction meetings, remote presentations, kickoff and alignment meetings, customer production meetings, and general discussions. Most frequently, MS Teams or Zoom is used to conduct these meetings.
How do I get to MMEC?
International travelers can arrive in China locally via Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG) or Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA). Visitors may also utilize regional airports such as Nantong Xingdong International Airport (NTG), Sunan Shuofang International Airport-Wuxi (WUX), and Changzhou Benniu International Airport (CZX). In addition, China Railway High-speed (CRH) operates bullet trains that connect all major hubs and make stops at Zhangjiagang Railway Station, a 40-minute drive from MMEC. (See Map Below).
