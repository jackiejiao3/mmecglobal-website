MISSION STATEMENT

Modern Group's mission is to provide quality products for our customers, a safe working environment for our employees, and contribute as much as possible to environmental initiatives. Despite our Group's challenges over the past several years, we have continued to increase efficiency and add value to our projects. Modern Group strives to be a global leader in structural steel fabrication and integrated modular plant solutions.

CEO MESSAGE

The success of Modern Group is a result of collaborative planning, the hard work of our employees, and the loyal support of our many varied customers. There were no easy decisions in the past several years, so the survival and expansion of Modern Group companies have been impressive, and the company is grateful. It is an honor to lead Modern Group companies and shape global infrastructure. Every day the sun rises with new opportunities, allowing our teams to implement new strategies to reach the goal of being a best-in-class supplier.

Modern Group's priorities are health, safety, the environment, and driving continuous process improvements. The long-term goal is to increase production technology, enhance engineering services, and provide complete equipment for the evolving modular plant and structural steel industries.

From now on, all companies must be accountable for managing ecological impacts. All people must become environmentally aware and be willing to reduce pollution from all sectors. Furthermore, there must be compliance with clean strategies that prevent harmful emissions, global warming, and the destruction of our ecosystems. As Modern Group changes, so too will the quality of life for our children and theirs. Embracing teamwork allows us to make progress even in the worst of times. We must lead by example and take responsibility for our actions. Ultimately, we will create a better place in a cleaner world and set a higher standard of stewardship—one step at a time.

George Jo, President / CEO

CORPORATE COMPLIANCE

Modern Group management has approved principles of conduct and compliance, advising our employees to act properly and make responsible choices.

CORE VALUES

Modern Group management embraces guiding principles and fundamental beliefs that foster teamwork and provide a better business experience.

  • HONESTY - Modern Group executives are truthful and sincere. We will not lie, cheat, or deceive our customers.
  • TRUST - Modern Group company has a business partnership concept and is always trustworthy.
  • COMMUNICATION - Modern Group management values clear, fluent, and timely communication, which prevents barriers or misunderstandings.
  • ADDING VALUE - Modern Group adds value with more services, more options, and supply chain flexibility.
  • PERFORMANCE - Modern Group performs responsibly as an organization within every department and process for all customers.
  • DILIGENCE - Modern Group employees work hard to meet deadlines. We are careful, persistent, and focused on the best results.
  • SOLUTIONS - Modern Group production teams regularly undertake and solve complex and technical problems.
  • QUALITY - Modern Group assures our products meet our customers' contractual design and quality expectations.
  • TRANSPARENCY - Modern Group is transparent in its business model, actions, management, and day-to-day operations.
  • ACCOUNTABILITY - Modern Group management is accountable for all actions, decisions, policies, products, and obligations to our customers.

GOVERNANCE

Modern Group's approach to corporate governance is a set of values and behaviors that underpin day-to-day operational activities. Modern Group executives endeavor to promote transparency, fair dealing, and protection of our customer's and employees' interests. To this end, Modern Group maintains the highest standards of corporate governance, which we view as critical to the sustainability of our future business.

Considering the large and increasing number of Modern Group employees, our human resource managers and HSE play an ever-increasing role in implementing corporate governance policies and training personnel. Additionally, Modern Group executives review operations, employee conduct, and daily activities to ensure compliance with the company's legal and regulatory objectives.

Modern Group executives believe in openness, integrity, and creating the best possible working environment. See below for more details on Modern Group's corporate governance and policies, or visit the website Media page here.
