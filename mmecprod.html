Where Scale Meets Precision

The most demanding structural steel projects are often defined by size, weight, complexity, and precision. Heavy column sections, boiler girders, long-span trusses, stadium roof structures, and large industrial assemblies require more than production capacity - they require engineering understanding, welding expertise, strict quality control, and proven project execution.

This is where Modern's capabilities are best demonstrated.

With extensive experience in power plants, sports stadiums, high-rise buildings, and large process facilities, we are well positioned to support projects where structural complexity and fabrication challenges are central to success. Our workshops are designed to handle heavy and complex assemblies, while our teams focus on delivering quality, schedule certainty, and practical solutions for global clients.

Heat Recovery Steam Generator (HRSG)

PROJECTS
68
COUNTRIES
29

The Heat Recovery Steam Generator (HRSG) is the main component after the gas turbine in combined-cycle and cogeneration power plants. Modern Group is a valued supplier of HRSGs and has delivered components for over 125 units. Modern Group fabrication teams manufacture non-pressure part commodities for horizontal and vertical HRSGs. These plants fall under a range of designations, such as Integrated Gasification Combined Cycle (IGCC), Combined Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT), Natural Gas Combined Cycle (NGCC), Combined Cycle Power Plant (CCPP), and Cogeneration (COGEN), also called combined heat and power (CHP).

The services provided for HRSGs include cost estimating, material procurement, project management, structure modeling and detailing, drawing production, fabrication, coatings, international packing, and global logistics.

  • Main Structural Steel Column Lines, Moment Beams, and Suspension Girders.
  • Modular Duct and casing (Column on Casing) Fabrication.
  • Loose Panel Fabrication, Stud Welding, Pipe Passages.
  • Panel Insulation and Liner Installation (Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel).
  • Stair Towers, Platforms, Ladders, Handrails, Gratings, Support Structures.
