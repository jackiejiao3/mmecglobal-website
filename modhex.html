MMEC HEAT EXCHANGER DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM

Modern Group senior management is launching a total solution program in the heat exchanger market. Heat exchangers transfer heat between a source and a working fluid used in cooling and heating processes. The heat exchanger market is broad, with applications in petrochemical plants, petroleum refineries, natural-gas processing, power stations, chemical plants, space heating, refrigeration, air conditioning, and sewage treatment.

Modern Group has supplied numerous projects with heat exchangers for over a decade, including shop-built modules and site-assembled modules. During that period, Modern's scope was fabrication and supply only.

Modern Group is now engaged in Thermal Design, Mechanical and Equipment Design, Steel Structure Design, Finite Element Analysis, Piping Design, and Instrumentation & Control Systems Design. Please get in touch with us if your business is interested in cooperation in this area.
