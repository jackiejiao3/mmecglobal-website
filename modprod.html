The Future Is Modular

Modular plants are integrated, factory-built process facilities assembled from transportable modules. They are increasingly valuable across oil & gas, chemical, mining, and other heavy industries. By shifting work off site to a controlled fabrication environment, modular plants can reduce site costs, accelerate schedules, and lower on-site labor requirements. Modularization Strategy

Modular plants bring process systems, equipment, piping, and structural steel together in transportable, factory-built modules. This modularization approach can accelerate construction, reduce costs, improve schedule certainty, and strengthen quality control. A factory-built environment offers improved equipment and machinery, more tools, flexible work shifts, and less environmental impact. The core of MMEC operations is to provide prefabrication, preassembly, modularization, and off-site fabrication (PPMOF), which today can be found in almost every discipline of construction.
