INDUSTRIAL COOLING SYSTEM EQUIPMENT

Over the years, Modern Group has supplied over 20,000 tons of modular air-cooled condensers for large lng projects and several power plants. Engageing these projects has been inconsistent, and now we are doing something about that. Modern Group will add more production and engineering support for ACC customers in late 2023, with added marketing support for this niche product.

Many industries require cooling systems and equipment, such as:

Oil & Gas Plants
Onshore LNG Plants, Offshore FPSO Vessels.
Refining plants
Crude oil, gasoline, diesel fuel, heating oil, kerosene, liquefied petroleum gas, jet fuel, asphalt base, heating oil, and fuel oils.
Petrochemical Plants
Ammonia, urea, methanol, sulphur recovery, PTA, styrene, caprolactame, nitric & cyanhydric acid, acetic acid, propylene, ethylene, melamine, sulfuric acid, HDPE~LPDE.
Chemical Plants
